## 2.9.0
- Standard colour areas on every pet, in the same order: Body, Back, Belly, Front legs, Back legs, Paws, Tail, Eyes, Eye shine, Mouth, Tongue, Ear backs, Inner ears, Head, Muzzle, Nose.
- Special areas: Neck, Throat and Tail tip (wolf and cat), Whiskers (cat), Tail rings and Mask (raccoon).
- The raccoon has a back patch and ear backs; the cat has ear backs; the cat and raccoon have a little tongue.
- New areas start as the colour they had before, so saved palettes and older versions look the same.

## 2.8.8
- The saved palette list now folds away under a "My palettes (N)" header, so the menu takes less room. It remembers whether you left it open.

## 2.8.7

  with a gentle arch on top and the belly colour along the throat.

## 2.8.6
- The wolf and cat now sit like a real dog or cat: rump on the ground, the back legs folded with the knee
  up and forward and the feet flat on the ground pointing forward (they have a knee joint now), and a
  gentle breathing loop that no longer jumps when it repeats.

  mouth, tongue, eye shine and the cat's whiskers, as well as fur, back, belly, eyes, nose and inner ears.
  The colour menu shows only the parts your kind of pet has. Older palettes still work.
