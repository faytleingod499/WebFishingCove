# Playground

Games and toys to enjoy with everyone who has the mod, on any server: **Tag** and a **swing set**, with more on the way.

*This mod was created with the assistance of AI.*

## Tag

1. Click the little **star button** on the left of your screen (or press **J**) and choose **Start a round of Tag**. Drag the button to put it wherever you like.
2. Everyone nearby with Playground gets a notice and has **10 seconds** to join by pressing **Y**.
3. Everyone who joined is sent back to spawn and a **5 second countdown** begins.
4. One player is picked at random to be **it**. A glowing white star floats above their head, and they run **10% faster**. "It" has to wait out the countdown, so everyone else gets a head start.
5. Touch someone to make them it. You can't tag back the player who just tagged you for 3 seconds.
6. After **3 minutes** the round ends and everyone sees how long each player was it. **Least time wins.**

You can leave a round any time from the J menu. If whoever is it leaves, someone else is picked.

## Swing set

1. Open the Playground menu and choose **Place a swing set**. It goes up a few steps in front of you, facing you. Choose **Pick up your swing set** to take it down. Everyone can put up one.
2. Walk up to a seat and press **E** to sit. There are two seats, so bring a friend.
3. The swing sways a little on its own. Press **Space right at the top of each swing**, front or back, to pump and go higher. A bar at the bottom of the screen lights up when it's time. A press at the wrong moment costs a little height.
4. At full height, press **Space at the top of the forward swing** to let go and fly across the sky, just like a blue mushroom.
5. Press **E** to get off.

Players with Playground see the swing set. Players without it still see you sitting and swinging, just in mid air.

## Good to know

- Only players with Playground take part or see the star. Everyone else sees nothing unusual.
- Everything goes through the Steam lobby, so it works on any server and needs nothing from the host.
